Rise is fast but you can't move anything. Storyline lets you move everything
but takes all afternoon.

I got tired of choosing, so I built a third option. It's called Midline, and
an early version is live today.

It runs in the browser. You get Rise-style blocks (tabs, flashcards, quizzes)
on a Storyline-style slide with layers, states, a timeline and triggers.

What I'm happiest with: it opens Storyline .story files, and saves projects
back out as .story files that open in Storyline 360. Slides, layers,
triggers, narration with captions, and quizzes all make the trip.

It also publishes to SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004 and xAPI. I checked the output on
SCORM Cloud, Moodle and a real LRS.

It's a preview, so some things don't convert yet (result slides and video on
the way out to Storyline, for two). The tool tells you what it couldn't carry
across every time.
