An 80% pass mark on a 9-question quiz means the learner can miss one
question. Miss two and they fail.

Most of us never do that math. We type 80% into the box because it sounds
right, and the quiz goes to the LMS.

A percentage hides the real decision, which is how many misses you'll allow:

5 questions at 80% = 1 miss
9 questions at 80% = 1 miss
10 questions at 80% = 2 misses
20 questions at 80% = 4 misses

So before you set a pass mark, count the misses. Then ask whether that's
what a pass should mean for this course. A quick refresher and a compliance
sign-off probably shouldn't share a number.

What pass mark do you default to, and do you know how many misses it allows?
