I make a Storyline quiz template called Arcade. The question I get most
about it is "how do I change the pass mark?"

So I wrote it up: how the score is worked out, where the pass mark is set,
and what to check before you publish.

One thing from the post that applies to any quiz, not only mine. Arcade has
nine scored questions and an 80% pass mark. That sounds generous, but it
means a learner can get only one question wrong. Two wrong and they fail.

It's worth doing that count on your own quizzes. Take the pass mark, work out
how many wrong answers it allows, and see if that's what you meant.
